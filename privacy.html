Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Data Protection Standard

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is a top priority for the Hybra Void development team. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you interact with Hybra Void on Discord, how that data is processed, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

Hybra Void operates on a strict data-minimization principle. We only collect and store technical identifiers necessary to provide bot functionality:

  • Discord Identifiers: User IDs, Guild (Server) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
  • Configuration Data: Server-specific settings such as log channel assignments, FireProof security status, AI moderation toggles, and TempVoice hub IDs.
  • User Activity Metrics: Moderation warning counts, XP levels, virtual currency balances (Hybra Credits), daily reward timestamps, and invite tracking counts.

We never collect passwords, email addresses, IP addresses, billing details, or direct messages outside of active support tickets.

3. Message Content & Third-Party AI Processing

To power automated profanity detection and AI responses (/ask, /roast, and bot mentions), message text is transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS requests to our AI inference provider (Groq API). Message content is processed in real time solely to generate a moderation verdict or reply and is never permanently logged or stored in our databases. If a violation occurs, the deleted message content may be sent directly to your server's designated private log channel for administrator review.

4. Data Storage & Security

All persistent configuration and user progression records are stored securely on isolated server infrastructure with restricted administrative access. We implement reasonable technical safeguards to protect stored identifiers against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We never sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

5. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

Data is retained only as long as Hybra Void remains active in a server or as required to maintain your user profile. Under global privacy standards (including GDPR principles), you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of the data stored associated with your Discord User ID.
  • Request complete deletion/reset of your stored warnings, XP, credits, or server configurations.

To exercise your data rights, open a support ticket in our official HYBRA'S RISE Discord Server, and our team will process your request within 72 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

In accordance with Discord's Terms of Service, Hybra Void is not intended for individuals under the minimum age required to use Discord in their respective country (typically 13 years old). We do not knowingly collect data from underage users.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we introduce new systems and commands. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
